# Welcome

This is a piece of writing. You are looking at it in the same place you will
write everything else, so the quickest way to learn this app is to read on.

Everything you write is saved automatically, the moment you stop typing. There
is no save button and you cannot lose work by closing the window.

## Making words look different

Select a word and press **Cmd+B** for bold, or **Cmd+I** for italic. On Windows
and Linux, use Ctrl instead of Cmd.

You will notice that bold text is wrapped in asterisks, like `**this**`. That is
how the text is stored, and it is what lets your writing move between this app,
your website, and anyone else's computer without breaking. You never have to
type those asterisks yourself — use the shortcuts, or the **Formatting** button
at the top of the window.

## Headings

A line that begins with a single `#` is the title of the piece. Two `##` marks a
main section. Three `###` marks a smaller one. The **Formatting** button will add
these for you.

## Focus mode

Press **Cmd+Alt+Z** (Ctrl+Alt+Z on Windows and Linux). The page moves to the
middle of the window, everything except the paragraph you are writing fades
away, and the line you are typing on stays in a comfortable place on screen
instead of sliding to the bottom.

Press it again to come back.

## Your writing lives in the list on the left

Every piece you write appears there, most recent first. The **+** button starts
something new. Right-click any piece to rename it, make a copy, or move it to the
trash.

You can show and hide that list with **Cmd+Alt+B** (Ctrl+Alt+B on Windows and
Linux).

## When you are ready to publish

Right-click anywhere in your writing and choose **Copy for pasting into a
website**. Then paste into your blog, your newsletter, or a document, and the
headings, bold text, and links all arrive intact.

There is also **Save as a web page** if you would rather hand someone a file.

## A note on spelling

Misspelled words get a coloured underline. Right-click one for suggestions. If
it is a name or a word you use often, choose **Add this word to my dictionary**
and it will not be flagged again.
