## One place for everything you write

Pick a single place on your computer where your writing is kept. Everything you
write will go there, newest first, so nothing gets lost.

The suggested place is a **Writing** folder inside your **Documents**. If you
keep your files in iCloud, Dropbox, or OneDrive, choose a place inside those
instead and your writing will follow you between computers.

You can move it later without losing anything.
