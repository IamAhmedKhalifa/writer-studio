## Three shortcuts and you are done

| What you want | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Bold | **Cmd+B** / Ctrl+B |
| Italic | **Cmd+I** / Ctrl+I |
| Focus mode on or off | **Cmd+Alt+Z** / Ctrl+Alt+Z |

Three more, once those feel natural:

| What you want | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Start a new piece | **Cmd+Alt+N** / Ctrl+Alt+N |
| All formatting options | **Cmd+Alt+M** / Ctrl+Alt+M |
| Show or hide the list of your writing | **Cmd+Alt+B** / Ctrl+Alt+B |

Everything here is also on the **Formatting** button at the top of the window, so
there is nothing to memorise.
